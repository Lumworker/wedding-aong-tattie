# Sprites

Pixel guests, trees, and flowers share one chunky pixel size so they sit with the couple and the chapel instead of turning into soft busts.

## Couple and chapel

- `groom.png` / `bride.png` — idle chibi portraits of อ๋อง and แทตตี้, original Game Boy Emerald–style art made for this wedding.
- `groom-wave.png` / `bride-wave.png` — matching wave strips. Four 256×256 frames in a row, with a 4px transparent gutter (and the same pad around the sheet) so frames do not bleed. The sheet is 1044×264.
- `groom-run.png` / `bride-run.png` — individual run strips, same 256×256 frame layout as the wave sheets (1044×264). Kept in the repo; the splash uses the shared strip instead.
- `groom-jump.png` / `bride-jump.png` — welcome hop drawn for this wedding. Sheet 1576×264: six 256×264 cells, 8px gutter between cells, no outer pad. Order: stand, crouch, rise, peak, descend, land. Same faces as the idle portraits. Not a hip-bump.
- `groom-peek.png` / `bride-peek.png` — head-and-shoulders peeks for the registration cards (438×537 and 549×552, transparent). Same faces. Hands grip the card’s top edge. Not a hip-bump.
- `couple-run.png` — both of them running together, facing right. Four 384×256 frames, 4px transparent gutter, sheet 1556×264. This is the loading splash.
- `church.png` — updated chapel, 428×605. Original art for this wedding. Replaces the earlier 110×165 crop.
- `church-silhouette.png` — distant sage chapel behind the welcome arch, 404×605.
- `flower-arch.png` — rose arch, 585×615.
- `ground-flowers.png` — corner rose bushes, 671×404.
- `wedding-bell.png` — bell with a blush bow, 397×622.
- `tree.png` / `tree-2.png` — round-canopy trees for the welcome lawn.
- `welcome-landscape.png` — welcome plate, 1280×720 RGB. Sage meadow, warm cream clouds, lavender hills. Original art for this wedding. Not used on the register step.
- `followers.png` — 8 small companion people (ผู้ติดตาม). Generated by `scripts/build-followers.py` from an AI-assisted concept (`vendor/concepts/followers-concept.jpg`, made for this site): pixelized on an 8-px grid, background keyed out, limited to 20 colors. Each 17×26 frame has a transparent gutter and faces the camera. Not from a third-party pack.

## Guests

`guests.png` is one 2×6 atlas built by `scripts/build-guests.py` from `vendor/concepts/guests-sheet-raw.png` (original art made for this wedding).

- Top row, indices 0–5: male guests. Bottom row, indices 6–11: female guests.
- One pose per cell. Cells are 177×309 with a 2px transparent gutter. The sheet is 1062×618.
- Background removal floods from the image edges through near-pure black only (max channel ≤ 2), so black and navy suits stay opaque. Characters are not redrawn or recolored.
- The art faces slightly right. A guest cell draws at 80 scene units, and followers stay about 60% of that height.

`guests-male.png` and `guests-female.png` are no longer shipped. Pixel People sources stay in `vendor/cc0-pixel/pixel-people/` for the retired compositor in `scripts/build-pixel-atlases.py` (CC0 1.0, https://opengameart.org/content/pixel-people). They are not the venue guests.

## Trees and plants

`plants.png` is the same script. It packs:

- **Pixelled trees** by Robert Ramsay — oak, birch, willow, pine, and maple, nearest-neighbor reduced from the 4× prestretched sheet so the pixels match the guests.
  - https://opengameart.org/content/pixelled-trees
  - License: **CC0 1.0**
  - Source: `vendor/cc0-pixel/trees/pixelledtrees.png`
- **Flowers** by SpiderDave — 12×24 blooms and grass tufts (mushrooms from that strip are not used).
  - https://opengameart.org/content/flowers
  - License: **CC0 1.0**
  - Source: `vendor/cc0-pixel/flowers/plants.png`

Frame positions are recorded in `src/lib/plant-atlas.ts`. Rebuild with:

```bash
python3 scripts/build-guests.py
python3 scripts/build-pixel-atlases.py
python3 scripts/build-followers.py
```
