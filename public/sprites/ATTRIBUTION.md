# Sprites

Pixel guests, trees, and flowers share one chunky pixel size so they sit with the couple and the chapel instead of turning into soft busts.

## Couple and chapel

- `groom.png` / `bride.png` — idle chibi portraits of อ๋อง and แทตตี้, original Game Boy Emerald–style art made for this wedding.
- `groom-wave.png` / `bride-wave.png` — matching wave strips. Four 256×256 frames in a row, with a 4px transparent gutter (and the same pad around the sheet) so frames do not bleed. The sheet is 1044×264.
- `groom-run.png` / `bride-run.png` — the same strip layout, used on the loading splash. Four frames facing right.
- `church.png` — chapel cropped from the user-supplied town building sheet.
- `cats.png` — 4 companion cat variants for followers. Each cat sits in its own frame with a transparent gutter so the next cat does not show through when the sprite is scaled.

## Guests

`guests-male.png` and `guests-female.png` are generated by `scripts/build-pixel-atlases.py`.

Each sheet is 6×4 characters, two frames wide (stand, then a one-pixel step). Bodies, hair, tops, and bottoms come from **Pixel People** by TokyoGeisha:

- https://opengameart.org/content/pixel-people
- License: **CC0 1.0** (public domain — commercial use, no attribution required)

Clothes are recolored into a small wedding palette (ivory, blush, navy, black, sage, gold). Skin tones are the pack’s natural peach and brown. Source sheets live in `vendor/cc0-pixel/pixel-people/`.

## Trees and plants

`plants.png` is the same script. It packs:

- **Pixelled trees** by Robert Ramsay — oak, birch, willow, pine, and maple, nearest-neighbor reduced from the 4× prestretched sheet so the pixels match the guests.
  - https://opengameart.org/content/pixelled-trees
  - License: **CC0 1.0**
  - Source: `vendor/cc0-pixel/trees/pixelledtrees.png`
- **Flowers** by SpiderDave — 12×24 blooms and grass tufts (mushrooms from that strip are not used).
  - https://opengameart.org/content/flowers
  - License: **CC0 1.0**
  - Source: `vendor/cc0-pixel/flowers/plants.png`

Frame positions are recorded in `src/lib/plant-atlas.ts`. Rebuild with:

```bash
python3 scripts/build-pixel-atlases.py
```
